Our Story

CasaNest began with a simple belief — that a home should feel calm, considered, and unmistakably yours.

Since 2018

Considered design, made to be lived with

We started in a small studio with a frustration: beautiful furniture was either disposable or unaffordable. So we set out to make pieces that sit somewhere better — honestly made, fairly priced, and built to age gracefully in a real home.

Today we work directly with makers who share that care, cutting out the middle so quality reaches you without the markup.
